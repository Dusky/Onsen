Desktop — same components, one sidebar, one rail

Nothing is redesigned for the big screen. The mobile composer stack unrolls sideways: the cast cards leave the composer and become a permanent right rail (where they can finally show relationship state and last line), the OPS grid stops being a sheet and becomes a labelled row, and the left sidebar is the mobile tab bar turned vertical. The prose column stays a reading measure — roughly 68 characters — because widening it would wreck the one thing the app is for.

Vocabulary fixed while I was in here: no more INT. RIDGE STATION — 03:40 slug lines. That was screenplay costume. The header now says what a roleplayer actually wants to know — who's in it, how long it's run, where you left off.
